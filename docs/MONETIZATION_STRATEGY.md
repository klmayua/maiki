## I. COMMISSION MODEL

### 1.1 Structure

**Transaction Fees:**
```
Client pays: $100
Platform fee: 15% ($15) - split 10% from client, 5% from VA
VA receives: $85
Platform keeps: $15
```

**Tiered Commission (by volume):**

| Monthly GMV | VA Commission | Client Commission | Total |
|-------------|---------------|-------------------|-------|
| $0-$1,000 | 10% | 5% | 15% |
| $1,001-$5,000 | 8% | 4% | 12% |
| $5,001-$20,000 | 6% | 3% | 9% |
| $20,000+ | 5% | 2% | 7% |

**Rationale:** High-volume power users get discounts, increasing loyalty.

---

### 1.2 Implementation

**Automatic Calculation:**
- Fees deducted at payment time
- Transparent fee display before checkout
- No hidden charges

**Payment Methods Supported:**
- Credit/Debit cards (Stripe)
- PayPal
- Bank transfer (ACH/Wire)
- Stablecoins (USDC, USDT)
- $MAIKI token (5% discount to incentivize)

---

### 1.3 Revenue Projection

**Year 1:**
- Target GMV: $2M
- Average take rate: 15%
- Commission revenue: $300K

**Year 2:**
- Target GMV: $15M
- Average take rate: 13% (tiered discounts)
- Commission revenue: $1.95M

**Year 3:**
- Target GMV: $50M
- Average take rate: 12%
- Commission revenue: $6M

---

### 1.4 Competitive Positioning

| Platform | Commission | Notes |
|----------|------------|-------|
| Upwork | 20% | High, but established |
| Fiverr | 20% | Standard rate |
| Belay | 50%+ | Premium, managed service |
| Maiki | 15% | Competitive, tiered |

**Value Proposition:** Lower fees + more features = better net earnings for VAs

## III. VAaaS (VIRTUAL ASSISTANT AS A SERVICE)

### 3.1 The Concept

**Maiki acts as the employer:**
- Client contracts with Maiki for outcomes
- Maiki manages delivery (AI + human VAs)
- Maiki guarantees quality and timeline
- Client pays premium for convenience

**The Value Stack:**
```
Client Need: "Manage my calendar and email"
<-- Maiki delivers -->
- AI agent handles routine scheduling
- Human VA handles complex coordination
- Escalation system for edge cases
- 24/7 coverage through time zones
- Guaranteed response times
- Replacement if VA unavailable
```

---

### 3.2 Pricing Tiers

**Starter VAaaS ($799/month):**
- 40 hours of VA work
- AI agent assistance included
- Email/calendar management
- Basic data entry
- Standard support
- Replacement guarantee

**Professional VAaaS ($1,499/month):**

**Executive VAaaS ($2,999/month):**
- 160 hours (full-time equivalent)

- Executive assistance

**Enterprise VAaaS (Custom):**
- Custom hours/packages
- Industry-specialized VAs
- Custom AI training

### 3.3 Cost Structure

## V. PRICING PSYCHOLOGY

### 5.1 Anchoring Strategy

**Three-Tier Structure:**
- Free: Gets users in the door
- Pro: "Most Popular" - where most users should land
- Enterprise: "For serious users" - anchors high value

**Visual Design:**
- Pro tier highlighted with "Best Value" badge
- Monthly/Annual toggle (annual saves 2 months)
- Feature comparison table shows Pro advantage

---

### 5.2 Decoy Effect

**VA Pricing:**
```
Pro:      $29/month
Agency:   $99/month  ← Most choose this (decoy makes it look reasonable)
Enterprise: $299/month
```

The $99 Agency tier looks affordable compared to $299, but expensive compared to competitors.

---

### 5.3 Loss Aversion

**Trial Periods:**
- 14-day free trial of Pro features
- At end: "You're about to lose: Priority matching, Analytics, Support"
- Convert freemium to paid before trial ends

## IX. RISKS & MITIGATIONS

### 9.1 Pricing Pressure

**Risk:** Competitors undercut our prices

**Mitigation:**
- Differentiation through features, not just price
- Loyalty programs reduce switching
- Value-based selling (ROI focus)

---

### 9.2 Churn Spike

**Risk:** Economic downturn causes mass cancellations

**Mitigation:**
- Freemium provides value at zero cost
- Pause option instead of cancellation
- Essential vs. nice-to-have feature categorization

---

### 9.3 Payment Fraud

**Risk:** Chargebacks eat into revenue

**Mitigation:**
- Escrow system reduces disputes
- Fraud detection algorithms
- Insurance reserve fund

---

### 9.4 Token Volatility

**Risk:** $MAIKI price swings make revenue unpredictable

**Mitigation:**
- Stablecoin options for core business
- Token revenue is "bonus" not "base"
- Treasury management for crypto assets

## XII. CONCLUSION

Maiki's diversified monetization strategy provides:

1. **Stability:** Multiple revenue streams reduce dependency
2. **Growth:** Each stream compounds over time
3. **Optionality:** Can double down on what works
4. **Competitive Moat:** Harder to copy than single revenue model

**The Path to $21.5M (Year 3):**
- Start with commission (immediate revenue)
- Build subscription base (recurring revenue)
- Launch VAaaS (high-margin revenue)
- Add token layer (community-aligned revenue)

**Key Success Factors:**
- Price for value, not cost
- Invest in retention (LTV > CAC)
- Monitor unit economics religiously
- Adjust based on data, not assumptions

**Maiki doesn't just monetize transactions. It monetizes trust, growth, and the future of work.**
